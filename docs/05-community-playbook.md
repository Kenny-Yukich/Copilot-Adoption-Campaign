# Internal Community Playbook

For running an internal AI/Copilot community (Viva Engage, Teams channel, or intranet forum). The mechanics generalize; the platform doesn't matter much.

## The honest math

Roughly: 1% of members post regularly, 9% react or comment, 90% read only. **This is normal and it is not failure.** A community where 90% read the weekly tip is doing its job. Optimize for reach and usefulness first, participation second.

## Seeding: the first three weeks

An empty community is a dead community. Before you invite anyone broadly:

1. Post 5–8 pieces of content so a newcomer arrives at something worth reading
2. Recruit 3–4 people privately who agree to comment on the first few posts — not fake enthusiasm, just a commitment to engage
3. Then invite the wider group

Nobody joins a conversation they'd be starting.

## Content mix that sustains itself

| Type | Cadence | Why it works |
|---|---|---|
| Prompt of the week | Weekly | Lowest-effort, highest-reach. This is the backbone |
| Champion spotlight | Biweekly | Peer proof. Converts skeptics better than anything you write |
| Myth vs. fact | Monthly | Absorbs the anxiety questions people won't ask publicly |
| Poll | Biweekly | Cheapest possible participation — one tap |
| Ask-me-anything thread | Monthly | Surfaces the questions your training missed |
| Wins roundup | Monthly | Feeds directly into your leadership reporting |

Templates in `templates/comms/`.

## Tactics that measurably move the needle

- **@mention specific people, sparingly.** A named ask converts far better than a broadcast. Overuse burns out fast — treat it as a scarce resource.
- **Polls to convert lurkers.** A voter is more likely to comment next time. It's a ladder, and voting is the first rung.
- **Reply to every single post for the first 90 days.** Unanswered posts teach people not to post.
- **Post when your audience is actually at a computer.** In a plant environment this may be shift-start or shift-end, not 9am. Check your own analytics rather than trusting general advice.
- **Credit people by name for wins, always with permission.** Ask first, every time.

## Health signals

Leading (watch weekly): unique posters, replies per post, time-to-first-reply, poll participation rate.
Lagging (watch monthly): reach as a share of members, wins submitted, and whether questions have shifted from "is this safe" to "how do I do X."

That last shift is the real indicator that the program has landed.

## When it stalls

Almost always one of three causes: you stopped posting, you stopped replying, or the content became announcements instead of usefulness. Diagnose in that order — it's usually the third.
