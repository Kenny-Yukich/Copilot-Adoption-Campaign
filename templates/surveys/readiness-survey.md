# Pre-Rollout Readiness Survey

**Purpose:** identify candidate use cases, find volunteers, and get a baseline you can point to later. **Not** to gauge enthusiasm.

**Length:** 8 questions max. Anything longer and your response rate collapses in a plant environment.
**Anonymity:** make it optional-name. You need names for volunteers, but honest answers require the option not to give one.

---

1. **What's your department?** *(single choice)*

2. **Which of these do you do at least weekly?** *(multi-select — this is the most important question in the survey)*
   - Write or edit documents
   - Summarize or read long documents someone else wrote
   - Process a high volume of email
   - Attend meetings where notes or follow-ups matter
   - Reconcile or compare data between sources
   - Build recurring reports
   - Answer the same questions repeatedly
   - Mostly work in one specialized system all day
   - Mostly hands-on / not at a computer

3. **What task eats the most time in your week for the least value?** *(free text — your richest source of use cases)*

4. **Roughly how many hours a week does that task take?** *(number — this is your baseline; you will want it in six months)*

5. **Have you used an AI assistant before, at work or personally?** *(Never / Tried it once / Occasionally / Regularly)*

6. **What's your biggest concern about AI tools at work?** *(free text — do not offer multiple choice here. The unprompted answers are what you need to address in training, and they will not match your assumptions.)*

7. **Would you be willing to try it early and give honest feedback, including negative?** *(Yes / No / Maybe — ask me)*

8. **Name (optional):**

---

## Using the results

- Q2 tells you who to license first. Heavy document and email roles first; single-system and hands-on roles later with a different approach.
- Q3 + Q4 are your before-metrics. **Keep them.** This is the data that makes your ROI case defensible instead of theoretical.
- Q6 sets your first five minutes of training. Address the top two concerns verbatim, out loud, before demonstrating anything.
- Q7 is your pilot cohort and your first champions.

## Post-training pulse (3 questions, one week after each session)

1. Have you used it on real work since the session? *(Yes, several times / Yes, once / No)*
2. If no, what stopped you? *(free text)*
3. What's the one thing that would make this more useful to you? *(free text)*

Question 2 is the only one that will change your program. Read every answer.
