# 2-Week Challenge Kickoff

The most reliable tool for converting the passive middle — people who attended training, agreed it was interesting, and haven't opened it since.

## Design

- **Two weeks.** One week is too short to build a habit; a month loses attention.
- **Team-based, by department.** Individual leaderboards demotivate everyone except the leader.
- **Score participation, not volume.** Count *people who used it on at least three days*, not total prompts. Volume scoring rewards waste and teaches people to game it.
- **Small prizes.** Lunch for the winning department, a trophy that migrates. The stakes should be visibly light — heavy stakes make it feel like a performance review.
- **Daily prompt drop.** One prompt per weekday in the community. This is the actual mechanism; the competition is just the reason to read it.

## Running it

- Post standings mid-week 1 and mid-week 2. Two updates is enough; daily standings get ignored.
- If a department is at zero by day 3, talk to their manager privately. Usually it's a scheduling or access problem, not resistance.
- Capture wins as they surface — this is your best two weeks of the year for spotlight material.
- Publish a closing post with the aggregate result and the three most-used prompts. Those three go straight into your automation backlog.
