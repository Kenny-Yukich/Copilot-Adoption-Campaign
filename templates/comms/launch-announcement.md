# Launch Announcement

**Channel:** email to the license cohort + community post
**Length target:** under 250 words. It will not be read past the first screen.

---

**Subject:** Copilot is live for your team — here's your first 10 minutes

Starting [DATE], you have Microsoft 365 Copilot in [APPS]. Here's what to do with it and what you should know.

**The three things worth trying first**

1. Open a long document you didn't write and ask it to summarize the parts relevant to you.
2. Open an email thread you inherited and ask what's outstanding and who owes what.
3. Take something you write repeatedly and ask it to draft the first version.

**What it can and can't see**

It can access what you already have permission to access — nothing more. It does not open files you couldn't already open, and it doesn't share your content with your colleagues.

**What it gets wrong**

It's confident when it's wrong. It invents details, especially names, numbers, and dates. **You own anything you send.** Check the facts before it leaves your hands.

## Notes on why this works

- Leads with an action, not a capability list
- Handles the data-access question before anyone has to ask it publicly
- States limitations plainly, which buys credibility for everything else
- The last paragraph pre-frames license reclamation as a service rather than a punishment
