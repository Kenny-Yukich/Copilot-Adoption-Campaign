# Weekly Tip Email / Prompt of the Week

**Cadence:** same day, same time, every week. Skipping weeks is what kills these.
**Length target:** under 150 words. One prompt. One idea.

---

**Subject:** Prompt of the week: [WHAT IT DOES IN 5 WORDS]

**The problem:** [ONE SENTENCE ABOUT A TASK PEOPLE ACTUALLY DO]

**The prompt:**

> [PASTE PROMPT WITH BRACKETED PLACEHOLDERS]

**Where to run it:** [APP]

**Why it works:** [ONE SENTENCE — usually about a specific constraint in the prompt]

**Watch out for:** [ONE HONEST LIMITATION]

Tried it? Reply and tell me how it went — good or bad. Full library: [LINK]

---

## Notes

- One prompt per email. Two prompts halves the odds either gets used.
- The "watch out for" line is not optional. It's the reason people trust the next one.
- Rotate departments so nobody goes more than a month without one aimed at their work.
- Reuse the same prompts in the community with the department tag. Cross-posting is not laziness — most people see only one channel.
