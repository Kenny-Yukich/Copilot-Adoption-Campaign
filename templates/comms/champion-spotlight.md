# Champion Spotlight

**Cadence:** biweekly
**Channel:** community post (with a link from the weekly email)
**Rule:** always get explicit permission before naming someone. Every time, no exceptions.

---

**Title:** How [NAME] in [DEPARTMENT] got [SPECIFIC OUTCOME]

**The task:** [WHAT THEY DO — in their language, not yours]

**Before:** [HOW IT WORKED. Include the time cost if they'll share it.]

**What they did:** [WHAT THEY ASKED COPILOT TO DO. Include the actual prompt if they'll share it.]

**After:** [THE RESULT. Concrete. Minutes, not adjectives.]

**In their words:**

> [ONE OR TWO SENTENCE DIRECT QUOTE — verbatim, approved by them]

**Steal this:** [THE GENERALIZED VERSION OTHER PEOPLE CAN USE TODAY]

---

## Notes on gathering these

- The best ones come from overheard remarks, not from a survey. When someone mentions a win in passing, write it down immediately.
- Ask three questions: what were you doing, how long did it take before, how long now. That's the whole interview.
- Choose subjects for **relatability over enthusiasm.** A skeptical veteran who grudgingly admits it saved them an hour is worth ten enthusiastic early adopters.
- Never inflate the numbers. The subject's colleagues know what their job takes, and one exaggeration discredits the series.
- Spread across departments and shifts. If every spotlight is an office worker, half your organization concludes it isn't for them.
